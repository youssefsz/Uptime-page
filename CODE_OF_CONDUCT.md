# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Uptime Page should be a welcoming, practical place to collaborate. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Communicate with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give and receive technical feedback constructively.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and evidence, not individuals.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing the project, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer using the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/youssefsz). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe, constructive community.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, impact, and whether harmful behavior is repeated. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately.
